# AI use policy

AI may assist retrieval, triage, extraction, deduplication suggestions, code, and documentation. Every proposed research annotation identifies its AI origin, model information available to the operator, procedure, date, source packet, and review status. Unknown model build details remain unknown.

Authoritative initial labels for the primary study are produced by eligible independent human assessors. An AI-generated suggestion reviewed by a human must disclose that exposure and cannot count as an unassisted independent rating. AI systems cannot be counted as human raters.

This first implementation and its literature screening were AI-assisted in Codex. Search results and opened source pages support a preliminary review, not an independently screened systematic review. Synthetic labels are explicitly marked; none represent human research judgments.

Archive material prompts and outputs under access and rights controls when they affect research reproducibility. Do not put credentials, unnecessary personal data, or sealed holdout packets in public logs.
