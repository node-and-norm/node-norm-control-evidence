# Source policy

Discovery resources identify candidates. They do not establish control operation. Preserve their record IDs, then seek original investigations, technical artifacts, contemporaneous operational records, and other proposition-relevant evidence.

Each source carries its URL, source date or explicit unknown, retrieval time, version or unknown, rights status, and source family. Each evidence item identifies a source and precise locator, plus the time to which its content applies. Hash only bytes actually retrieved; a bibliographic record is not an archived document.

Relations represent derived_from, aggregation_of, reports_same_claim, independent_corroboration, supersedes, correction_of, and contradicts. A primary_record is a source role, not an edge to a fictitious event document. Derivative and aggregation edges point from the later representation to its upstream source. Corrections point to the earlier object. Independence requires an explicit rationale about acquisition and underlying assertion, not different publisher names. Unknown dependence stays unknown.

The MIT tracker explicitly applies an LLM pipeline to AIID reports. Preserve that lineage. OECD describes an aggregation and classification pipeline and disclaims independent verification of underlying reporting. Different databases may share the same news origin. [MIT methodology](https://airisk.mit.edu/ai-incident-tracker/incident-view); [OECD methodology](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents-methodology).

Evidence quality is assessed per proposition. A court filing can establish that an allegation was made without proving the allegation. An institutional statement can establish a declared safeguard without proving runtime behavior. Retain disagreement and subsequent correction.

Record search queries, accessed and inaccessible sources, dates, stopping reasons, and evidence cutoff for each packet. Apply a common retrieval budget in the availability cohort and report deviations. Later evidence creates a new packet version. Never overwrite the version used for prior coding.
