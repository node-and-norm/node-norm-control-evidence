## Finding

The [follow-up pressure test](PRESSURE_TEST_2026-09-14.md) records additional inspection of four works' methods, the remaining access gaps, and a proposed comparative pilot. In particular, the closest accountability paper explicitly distinguishes insufficient evidence in its method. The initial access levels below are retained as the historical first-pass record; the follow-up supersedes them where indicated.

The broad topics already have substantial prior work. Control operation is an established audit concern; AI safety cases connect claims to evidence; incident researchers study uncertainty and institutional response. Recent work directly examines post-deployment evidence and AI forensics. CEC must demonstrate incremental value through independently reproducible event-control coding, explicit source dependence and missingness, and validated reuse. Combining familiar features is not itself proof of a scholarly contribution.

## Closure protocol

Before asserting novelty, freeze databases, search strings, dates, languages, inclusion criteria, and screening roles. Export all result metadata; deduplicate by DOI, title, and version while preserving source records. Two human screeners independently assess the closest works and retain disagreement. Retrieve full methods, codebooks, schemas, data, and validation reports where available. Record access failures.

For each included work, code event-control unit, control-specific applicability, evidence/world-state separation, provenance dependencies, temporal correction, human authority, unresolved categories, independent coding, holdout, and reusable versioned data. Use present, absent with inspected evidence, or unresolved. An abstract's silence is unresolved. Follow backward and forward citations of the closest competitors.

The novelty hypothesis fails if an existing resource already provides the claimed method and validation to comparable scope. A useful result may then be a replication, interoperability contribution, or a measured improvement. Any resulting scope amendment must follow the Charter and Agenda change rules.
