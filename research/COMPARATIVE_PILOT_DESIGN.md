# Comparative pilot design: proposed protocol

[Repository](../README.md) / [Research](README.md)

This proposal operationalizes the governing validation agenda. It has not been frozen, preregistered, staffed, or run. Pilot results may change the design; every change must be logged before confirmatory use.

## Decision served

Determine whether the CEC representation improves independent reconstruction enough to justify its annotation cost. Preserve the approximately 30-event development pilot and provisional main-study planning target. This proposal adds a comparison within development; it does not change the governing research question.

## Comparators

Prepare three views of identical, rights-reviewed evidence packets: an incident narrative with source links; a simple control checklist with explicit unknown values; and the CEC event-control representation. Do not weaken the baselines by removing uncertainty or citations they can reasonably carry. Freeze construction rules and record preparation time for each view.

Use separate, balanced reviewer assignments across views so a reviewer does not learn a case from one representation before assessing another. Declare case familiarity and conflicts. Keep evidence content, retrieval cutoff, and access constant for this comparison. A later enrichment experiment may compare discovery-only and primary-enriched packets, but it must estimate that effect separately.

## Enumeration before coding

Give eligible reviewers event scope and source material without expected control labels. Have them independently identify control objectives, opportunities, and relevant periods. Preserve unmatched controls and disagreements about event boundaries. Define an explicit matching procedure and adjudicate matches separately from substantive dimension values.

Report event-level counts of proposed controls, matched and unmatched opportunities, and reasons for differences. Conditional dimension agreement among matched controls must be accompanied by the enumeration result. Otherwise the analysis hides an earlier source of measurement uncertainty.

## Candidate outcomes

Record the number of conclusions independently traceable to specific evidence, unsupported affirmative or negative conclusions, correct retention of unresolved states, time to reconstruct each case, and reviewer workload. Define scoring rubrics and missing-data treatment before assessment. Independent experts may assess documentary support, with conflicts and initial judgments preserved; expert consensus is not ground truth about the underlying event.

For known-state controlled examples, separately measure whether the reconstruction identifies the programmed control state. Report these results separately from retrospective cases. A synthetic software fixture is not a human criterion-validity study.

## Analysis and failure

Compare views within a balanced case design while accounting for event and reviewer dependence. Choose the primary outcome, minimum worthwhile difference, sample allocation, uncertainty procedure, and burden threshold before a confirmatory comparison. Pilot estimates inform feasibility and precision; do not use them as a confirmatory test of the same hypothesis.

The method may fail through no improvement over a simpler representation, higher unsupported certainty, unresolved enumeration instability, persistent missingness-category confusion, or prohibitive coding burden. Freeze the operational failure criteria before main-study use. Preserve and report failures. Do not retune on the sealed holdout.

## Unfilled requirements

Independent assessors, packet builders, documentary-support reviewers, and a holdout custodian remain unassigned. Source rights, case frame, time budget, scoring manual, and precision analysis remain unfinished. This document is a reviewable design, not evidence that any of those activities occurred.
