## Why inspect this source

This investigation offers concrete locations for separating a control's available interface, its recorded exercise, and its consequences. The research task is to retain those distinctions through coding.

The source is NTSB report **HAR-19/03**, adopted November 19, 2019; the inspected corrected copy records a June 26, 2020 title-page correction. It concerns the March 18, 2018 Tempe collision. [Official report](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/AccidentReports/Reports/HAR1903.pdf).
