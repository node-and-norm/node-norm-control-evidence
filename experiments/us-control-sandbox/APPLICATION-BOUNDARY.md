# First application boundary, candidate 0.1

A synthetic service-request system recommends whether to proceed with an action. An authored controller can issue a stop before commitment or correct a record afterward. The system stores an internal record and a recipient-facing copy. No money, email, external service, biometric data or actual customer is involved.

Decision unit: one synthetic request. The basic suite schedules commitment at logical tick 10 and a post-action correction at tick 12 where specified. The recovery suite orders stop and commit commands across separately launched worker processes, without a tick-based human-timing measure. A same-tick stop is processed after commitment. Stop success means no action commitment. Correction delivery means both copies reflect the change. A correction does not erase the prior action or establish remediation of real harm.

Actors: decision component (automated); scripted controller (automated, simulating a permitted or denied command); executor (automated); outcome observer (author-written software). There is no enrolled human reviewer, real institution or legally delegated authority. The boolean permission field is a simulation setting. It cannot establish a HIT named-human authority requirement or TAE exercised judgment.

The development set contains nine basic conditions and seven recovery conditions, with one deterministic execution per condition in each corresponding rehearsal. Cases are intentionally selected to expose different mechanisms. No frequency or population effectiveness estimate follows. Future held-out families must be designed and recorded before evaluation.

Current source connection: candidate failure mechanisms from the first screening tranche. NIST-MAPPING.md revision 0.1 identifies six selected provisions of AI RMF 1.0. No formal regulatory sandbox authority, agency partner or field deployment exists.

Allowed immediate output: traceable technical evidence packets. Method-level assessments and aggregate scores remain disabled. ADMISSIBILITY.md records why the current boundary permits technical appendices only. The current methods may require a later operational-capability exercise with authentic authority evidence; that is not supplied by inventing a human name in a synthetic fixture.
