# Contract review: technical evidence boundary

2 October 2026. Researcher-controlled, AI-assisted analysis. Pinned sources and hashes: references/manifest.json. This is an evidence map and does not assign findings.

The current HIT specification allows operational tests under sections 4.3, 5.3, 6.3 and 6.4. Those provisions require applicable named authority and usable mechanisms within a declared boundary. Our scripts have synthetic permissions; they do not establish institutional authority or human influence. The current pinned handbook and dimension catalog have now been reviewed. They preserve actor attribution, evidence-state classification before finding assignment, dimension-specific claims and locators, and separate integrity components. Schema validity would not resolve that evidence gap.

| HIT Reform | None | No declared follow-up, actor or architecture change |

TAE's five evidence states and HIT's 0/1/2/IE findings are distinct contracts. Missing evidence must retain its contract-specific treatment. No combined score or automatic conversion is justified.

Disposition: proceed with technical environment testing and source selection. Keep method scoring disabled. A future adapter can first package traceable artifacts with locators, explicit synthetic provenance, authority descriptions, missing-evidence declarations and transformation history. A complete conforming assessment requires a separately reviewed boundary and admissibility decision.

## Completed handbook/catalog review

The handbook sections 1–4 require the declared boundary, actor-authority matrix and bounded evidence claims before findings. Sections 5–7 distinguish missing evidence from affirmative absence and require qualifying harm before Repair findings. Catalog version 0.4.0 uses the same six dimensions and integrity ordering as the normative contract. Its non-claims include runtime enforcement and causal effectiveness. Disposition: evidence packaging may proceed; a synthetic controller cannot be presented as a named human authority. See APPLICATION-BOUNDARY.md.

## Applied decision

[ADMISSIBILITY.md](ADMISSIBILITY.md) records the next gate: permit technical evidence appendices, withhold full TAE/HIT findings under the present synthetic boundary, and preserve method-specific requirements. The [appendix builder](build_evidence_appendices.py) reads supplied packet fields without consuming private outcome records. This does not reinterpret operational-test provisions as a blanket prohibition on future properly bounded tests.
