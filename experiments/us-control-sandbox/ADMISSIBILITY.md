# Evidence admissibility decision, candidate 0.1

2 October 2026. AI-assisted development analysis under the inspected TAE and HIT contracts. This decision authorizes only the experiment's technical evidence appendix. It does not amend either method, grant institutional authority, freeze a confirmatory study or record independent human approval.

## Decision

The current sixteen scripted conditions are eligible for a technical evidence appendix. They are ineligible for a complete TAE practical-control conclusion or a conforming HIT assessment of human influence. Their software records can establish bounded facts about simulated execution. No person exercised the controller's commands, and no real institutional decision process is declared.

This is an eligibility decision before findings. Do not create a HIT record containing six `IE` findings merely because a synthetic example lacks humans. A conforming assessment first needs an applicable unit and boundary. Within an eligible assessment, missing evidence receives the treatment required by its contract. Likewise, do not assign TAE `outside scope` to remove required stages.

The absence of humans is explicit in the authored application design. It is a reason this experiment cannot substantiate human control, not an empirical finding that an institution lacked human authority.

## What the appendix may say

| Supplied record | Permitted observation | Inference withheld |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted stop response | The response record says the command was accepted. | That execution stopped or a human held authority. |
| Simulated action state | The supplied downstream record has this terminal state. | That the underlying institutional decision was correct or human control was effective. |
| Internal and recipient record states | The two recorded values agree or differ. | Substantive reconsideration, harm remediation or delivery to a real person. |
| Committed effect count | The supplied store-derived record contains this many effects for its listed requests. | Human intention, population risk or concurrent-system correctness. |
| Null downstream field | This packet does not supply a downstream result. | Failure, success or the reason the record is missing. |
| Matching artifact hash | The bytes match the selected run's recorded digest. | Truth, completeness, independent corroboration or protection against rewriting both artifact and manifest. |

The code in `build_evidence_appendices.py` applies this narrow extraction rule to the nine basic packets and seven recovery packets already published. It reads their supplied fields and recorded digests. It never reads `researcher-only/outcomes.json`, simulator databases or expected case labels. The output is a separate appendix format with scoring disabled; it is not the HIT schema or a TAE stage ledger.

## Evaluation plan before assessment

Keep three questions separate:

1. Did the authored software behave as its fixture specified? Existing run checks address this.
2. What can the supplied packet establish about that software behavior? The appendix preserves observable values and missingness without inferring absent outcomes.
3. Does TAE or HIT improve a justified assessment of practical human control? This remains untested and requires an admissible design.

For a later permitted technical-evidence exercise, define false reassurance as a favorable conclusion not supported by the supplied evidence, rather than any statement differing from an undisclosed known outcome. Missing evidence can coincide with a working control. Record separate counts for unsupported reassurance, missed supported faults and justified unresolved responses. Denominators, severity rules, ambiguous cases and the comparison with a simple checklist must be fixed before evaluation. No such rates are calculated here.

The current public fixtures and their labels are development material. Future withheld cases need genuinely new case families, separate custodianship/access where applicable, a fixed packet-generation policy and an answer key that records both actual behavior and what each packet can support. A known outcome alone is not an evidence-sufficiency answer key.

## Next gate and options

Continue without participants by evaluating packet preparation, documented technical effects and evidence insufficiency as an explicitly separate engineering study. This can expose weaknesses in the evidence pipeline while preserving the methods' human-control constructs.

A full TAE/HIT application needs an authentic, bounded process with appropriate authority and human evidence, or a separately proposed synthetic-method extension with its own claims and validation plan. The original HIT replication and TAE prospective protocol remain separate workstreams. No live institution, human-study permission or new scoring contract is created by this document.
