## First proposed source connection

The FTC complaint alleges that alert outcomes were not consistently recorded and problematic enrollments remained active. These are two distinct questions for a synthetic test: was the outcome recorded, and did a correction alter the active record? Do not assume that an acknowledgment answers either question. This inference guides future scenarios; the present simulator does not reproduce Rite Aid's system, facial recognition, staff behavior, discrimination, or harm.

The AIID entry is a discovery source. The FTC complaint supplies the bounded primary allegation. Repeated reporting of the same complaint is source dependence, not independent corroboration. Before historical assessment, review relevant pleadings, orders and competing evidence, define the cutoff, and preserve a complete source manifest.
