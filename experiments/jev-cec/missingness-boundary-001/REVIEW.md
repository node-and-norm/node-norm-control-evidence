# Pre-execution wording and reference review

All 32 proposed judgments are retained conditionally under documentary-support standard 001. The review found one packet wording problem: describing a withheld document as a record of an outcome “attributable to R” could imply that attribution was already established. Twelve withheld-record statements now say the record addresses whether the relevant event or effect occurred. No reference labels changed.

The [review register](REVIEW.json) records every card-dimension judgment, its revised evidence, the conditional disposition and all twelve before/after edits. It binds the revised cards and unchanged reference by hash. This is exposed AI-assisted review with access to the proposed answers, not independent adjudication. No live outputs exist for this design, so the clarifications occur before execution.

## Judgment boundaries

Issuance is explicitly reported for every card, supporting the eight proposed action labels. The other 24 proposals concern evidence availability: absent information supports not_reported; an explicit barrier to a relevant record supports not_observable under the experimental rule. A custodian's description establishes the asserted topic and restriction in the invented packet. It establishes neither the document's contents nor a successful intervention.

The receipt, execution and consequence propositions can be causally related in a real workflow. Here the test concerns documentary-support classifications, with separate availability statements. A restricted outcome record does not by itself establish restricted receipt records. Conversely, the standard allows an explicit barrier to relevant evidence to control the missingness label even when another causal link is absent. That precedence remains a construct assumption requiring later examination.

## Instruction comparison limits

The structured candidate shortens the instructions and expands two missingness criteria. It also omits detailed examples in the baseline concerning cancellation, settlement, partial coverage and no-handoff mechanisms. Those omitted examples are not varied by these packets, but their removal changes the instruction package. Any result must remain scoped to this package and these missingness cases. Do not attribute a difference solely to JSON structure or brevity.

The eight packets cover only three proposed labels: yes, not_reported and not_observable. They cannot validate the other five labels or support general superiority over the shared instructions. Explicitly stating absence and restriction creates a useful controlled test but supplies much cleaner evidence than typical institutional records. No claim about natural-document performance follows.

Option-order reversal also changes block position and package order. The design discloses this confounding and treats reversal as secondary sensitivity. It cannot isolate a causal option-order effect. A later design seeking that claim would require separately counterbalanced ordering and repetition.
