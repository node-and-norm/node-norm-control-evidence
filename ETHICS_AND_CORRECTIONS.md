# Ethics and corrections

Retain only personal information necessary to explain the event or establish provenance. Do not infer protected characteristics, motives, diagnoses, or private conduct. Distinguish allegations, source findings, and researcher inferences. Evaluate foreseeable harm before making a record public, especially for vulnerable people.

A correction request identifies affected IDs, the asserted error, and supporting evidence. A maintainer logs receipt, checks urgent privacy concerns, and assigns a reviewer without a relevant conflict. Preserve the original assertion and review trail in appropriate restricted custody when public minimization requires removal. Publish a versioned correction or a reasoned decision with bounded detail.

Corrections propagate through supersedes or correction_of relations and release notes. Notify downstream consumers through the release record. Interested parties can challenge evidence; they cannot silently erase supported findings. No external messages are sent automatically by this repository.
