# Sampling plan: development draft

The pilot targets approximately 30 events for definition development. Purposive variation should cover domains, control functions, source access, adverse events, near misses, and interventions. Pilot results are exploratory. Pilot events, their duplicates, and known downstream case derivatives cannot become untouched holdouts.

The main planning target is approximately 200 events. Final size requires a frozen frame, event-level clustering assumptions, expected control counts, retrieval capacity, missingness assumptions, and precision simulation. Counting 200 controls from a handful of events does not satisfy the planning rationale.

Stage A measures evidence availability in a stratified incident frame. Freeze discovery snapshots, eligibility dates, AI involvement criteria, geography/language coverage, deduplication decisions, strata, random seed, and selection probabilities before drawing. Retain sparse-evidence cases. Record all exclusions and replacements with reasons. Include a source-defined frame limitation in every estimate.

Stage B deepens analysis for cases passing a prespecified evidence threshold. The threshold and all inclusion decisions must be frozen after the pilot and before main coding. Publish the Stage A to Stage B flow and compare observed selection characteristics. Do not report Stage B proportions as representative of Stage A.

The principal candidate estimand for dimension d is the proportion of applicable event-control observations whose value resolves to yes or no under the frozen rule. Partial, conflicting, unknown, not_observable, and not_reported remain unresolved in this strict estimand. Report each separately and report the not_applicable count. Show both control-weighted and event-weighted summaries; the preregistration must designate the primary weighting. Zero denominators produce undefined results.

Uncertain control opportunities require a separate enumeration-coverage report. Events with no identifiable control opportunity stay in the event-level availability report. They must not disappear because a control-level denominator cannot be constructed.

An independent custodian seals a prespecified main-study holdout after deduplication. Split whole event families, including derivative case studies, before developers see packets. Freeze the fraction, IDs commitment, custody, access log, and unsealing rule. A hash alone does not establish non-access. Holdout size and custodian are unresolved; no holdout is claimed sealed.
