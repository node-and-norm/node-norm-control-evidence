# Data statement

This development version contains one synthetic event-control example and no empirical corpus records. It demonstrates the object model and safeguards. It cannot support estimates about incidents, organizations, human behavior, or control effectiveness.

Planned data comes from publicly documented events, enriched with primary evidence where available. Selection, reporting, language, access, publicity, and source-dependence biases limit inference. The initial study estimates observability within its declared frame and retrieval procedure.

The corpus preserves source assertions separately from annotations and adjudication. Missingness is explicit. Sensitive data is minimized, source rights remain separate, and corrections are versioned. See SOURCE_POLICY.md, SAMPLING_PLAN.md, and ETHICS_AND_CORRECTIONS.md.

Distribution is currently a development repository. There is no archived dataset release or DOI. Research citations must state the exact development revision and its infrastructure-only status.
